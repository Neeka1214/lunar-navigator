The problem & our approach

At the lunar south pole, the Sun skims the horizon

Real sky panorama from the Artemis III "Connecting Ridge" region. The Sun (yellow) stays about 1.5° high and slides along the real terrain horizon (grey); Earth (blue) bobs above and below it.
☀

Power: with the Sun only 1–2° high, a hill several km away can block it for days.

🌍

Comms: Earth sits on the horizon and rises and sets as the Moon wobbles (libration).

⏱

Replanning: when a launch slips, every site and date must be checked again, quickly.

👥

Users: mission planners, rover teams, students and anyone who loves looking at the Moon.

A Moon legend turns the science into a story and a game

🌕 How long would Chang'e's flight take?

Chang'e drank the elixir and floated to the Moon, where the Jade Rabbit keeps her company. The tool uses the real Earth–Moon distance for the chosen date from JPL Horizons: on 2026-11-14 it is 405,270 km centre to centre, about 397,162 km ground to ground.

🚶 Walking5 km/h9.1 years
🚄 Taiwan High Speed Rail300 km/h55.2 days
✈️ Airliner900 km/h18.4 days
🚀 Apollo 11 average (1969)5,300 km/h3.1 days
💡 Light300,000 km/s1.32 seconds
🌙 Chang'e: type any speedyour choice?

But wait… it is not that simple!

🌙

The Moon won't wait for her. It circles Earth at 3,680 km/h, 12× faster than the train, and goes around Earth about 2 times during her 55-day ride. She must aim where the Moon will be.

🌍

Earth's gravity pulls her back. She needs about 11 km/s (40,000 km/h), Earth's escape speed. At train speed she would never reach space.

📏

The distance keeps changing. The orbit is an ellipse (356,648 to 406,603 km), so the same train ride takes 48.4 to 55.3 days.

Real game screen at Artemis III Amundsen Rim: sunlit lunar ground under a starry sky, and a Jade Rabbit 130 m straight ahead. The purple box counts the rabbits and points to the nearest one.

The science hidden in the game: 3 of the 5 rabbits hide in the darkest, coldest spots within 4 km, picked from our NASA-data sunlight map. That is exactly where water ice can survive for billions of years. Players discover it by walking there.
