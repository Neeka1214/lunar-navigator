🐇 Game: find the Jade Rabbits (玉兔)

Five Jade Rabbits are hiding within about 4 km of the landing site. Three of them hide in the darkest, coldest spots, just like real water ice. Walk around on the real terrain and find them all. A box at the bottom left tells you how far away the nearest one is. The rabbits only appear while the hunt is on, so the 3D view stays clean for everyone else.
